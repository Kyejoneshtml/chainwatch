Standing safety information that is always true, not an alert about a specific event.

```jsx
<WarningBlock>
  Chainwatch will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys. Anyone who does is attempting to steal your funds.
</WarningBlock>
```

`surface-raised` fill, `border-strong` outline, `text-primary` body. Deliberately carries no severity colour — red is reserved for critical events, and spending it on permanent advice that is always on screen would dilute what red means when it does appear.
