Renders a Bitcoin address or transaction id: mono font, truncated in the middle (never at the end), with an adjacent copy control.

```jsx
<AddressLabel address="bc1qxy2kgdygjrsqtzq2n0yrf2493p83kkfjhx0wlh" />
```

Truncating in the middle preserves both the leading and trailing characters people use to visually match addresses.
